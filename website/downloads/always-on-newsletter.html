INTRODUCING ALWAYS ON

Personal.
By nature.

A more personal shopping experience.
Built into the one you already have.

Every store has its own rhythm. Customers browse, compare, choose a size and put a look together. We believe personalization should move with them.

That is the idea behind SPREEAI Always On: visualization, fit and styling woven into your existing commerce experience, online and in store.

▶   Watch the Always On introduction

One setup.
More possibility.

A shopper signs in through your account system and, with permission, adds a photo and fit details. Their saved profile can then power eligible product imagery, recommendations, sizing and comparisons as they shop.

They do not need to restart a separate try-on flow for every garment. Shoppers who are just exploring can start with a curated AI Twin.

Your brand sets the direction.

Choose the features and placements. Curate the AI stylist’s look, voice and point of view. Direct the poses and future movement that express each garment.

For existing partners, we start with your current SDK implementation and scope the account, profile and storefront additions together.

Explore Always On for your brand ↗

Always On is a proposed integration experience. Some capabilities, including AI stylist, brand-directed poses, simulated back views, video try-on and fit visualization, are concept or roadmap previews. Availability and scope will be confirmed with each partner. Images are creative illustrations.
