A NOTE TO OUR PARTNERS

Introducing
Always On.

A more personal shopping experience.
Built into the one you already have.

Every store has its own rhythm. Customers browse, compare, choose a size and put a look together. We believe personalization should move with them.

That is the idea behind SPREEAI Always On: visualization, fit and styling woven into your existing commerce experience, online and in store.

One setup.
More possibility.

A shopper signs in through your account system and, with permission, adds a photo and fit details. Their saved profile can then power eligible product imagery, recommendations, sizing and comparisons as they shop.

They do not need to restart a separate try-on flow for every garment. And customers who are just exploring can start with a curated AI Twin.

Your brand
sets the direction.

Choose the features and placements. Curate the AI stylist’s appearance, voice and point of view. Direct the poses and, as video try-on develops, the movement that best expresses each garment.

Build from
where you are.

For existing partners, we start with your current SDK implementation and scope the account, profile and storefront additions together. Always On is a proposed integration experience, not an automatic update to every existing deployment.

Brand-directed poses, simulated back views, AI stylist and fit visualization are concept or roadmap previews. Feature and category availability will be confirmed for each partner.
